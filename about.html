Who I Am

Kristian Bran

Aloha! I'm a Kauai-based creative professional specializing in videography, photography, and audio/visual production. With 5+ years of experience across the islands, I've covered everything from sports portraits and lifestyle sessions to large-scale live events and festival productions.

Whether I'm behind a camera capturing an athlete mid-throw, documenting a beach portrait session, or running sound for a live event — my focus is always the same: tell a story that people feel long after the moment passes.

I built QB Media Hawaii to be the single point of contact for clients who don't want to juggle five different vendors for one event. One call books your camera, your sound, your lighting, and your stream — all coordinated, all on time.
